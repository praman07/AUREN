Company Policy: AUREN Remote Work Guidelines
Clearance: Confidential
Department: Engineering, Human Resources

1. Core Working Hours
All employees in the Engineering and HR departments must be online and active between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST.

2. Equipment and Stipends
The company provides a $500 home office stipend for all remote workers to purchase ergonomic furniture or computer peripherals.

3. Security Protocol
All access to enterprise code repositories and the knowledge platform must go through the corporate Zero-Trust Network Access (ZTNA) gateway. Credentials must never be shared.
